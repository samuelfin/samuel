# Roadmap and Initial Cost Model

## Delivery roadmap

### Phase 0 — Product discovery (current)

- Establish scope assumptions, product requirements, architecture, data model, provider assessment, AI governance, regulatory dependencies, roadmap and preliminary cost drivers.
- Gate: SFI confirms launch coverage/customer priority, funding envelope and legal review path; provider rights are researched before live display.

### Phase 1 — Foundation

- Next.js/TypeScript application, design system, authentication, roles, organisation tenancy, PostgreSQL migrations, audit log, administration shell and global search foundation.
- CI/CD, staging, secret management, error monitoring, backup and restore procedure.
- Gate: access-control model and operational baseline reviewed.

### Phase 2 — Market and macro data

- Provider registry, ingestion jobs, provenance and data-quality review queue.
- Integrate one licensed NGX product after contract; add selected public macroeconomic release pipeline with confirmed reuse basis.
- Dashboard, equity/company pages, economic series and freshness indicators.
- Gate: signed terms, measured data quality, provider acceptance and stale-feed monitoring.

### Phase 3 — Financial research

- Versioned statements, financial ratios, period-safe comparisons, news/corporate actions, fixed income, report authoring and exports.
- Gate: source review, formula sign-off and FMDQ/issuer data permissions.

### Phase 4 — AI intelligence

- Approved corpus, retrieval, deterministic calculations, citations, document extraction/review, evaluations and usage controls.
- Gate: finance evaluation quality, legal review of user-visible responses, provider rights for AI use.

### Phase 5 — User services and launch

- Watchlists, alerts, configurable plans, selected payment provider, support and user analytics.
- Gate: operational readiness, legal and security approvals, customer support and incident response.

### Phase 6 — Institutional expansion

- Organisation workspaces, SSO options, API access, stronger tenant administration, advanced licensed datasets and service-level commitments.
- Gate: enterprise contracts, data entitlements and capacity plan.

## Cost model (planning ranges, not vendor quotes)

Currency: USD-equivalent planning estimates; local tax, FX movement and Nigerian vendor quotations can materially change totals. Amounts exclude founder opportunity cost and market-data licensing unless stated.

| Workstream | Initial planning range | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Product/UX discovery and prototype | $5k–$20k | Product definition, terminal interaction design, user validation |
| Foundation and secure application | $25k–$80k | Auth, tenancy, admin, schema, deployment and observability |
| Data ingestion and research modules | $30k–$100k | Provider adapters, validation, financial statements, reports and exports |
| AI/document intelligence | $20k–$80k | Retrieval, extraction, review workflow, evaluation and controls |
| Security, legal and operational readiness | $10k–$50k | Independent review, legal counsel, policies, backups and launch readiness |
| **Indicative build total** | **$90k–$330k** | Broad 6–12+ month range depending team, scope and vendor terms |
| Cloud/software operations | $500–$5k+/month | Early workloads; storage, observability, email and AI usage vary |
| Licensed market data | **Quote required** | NGX/FMDQ packages, display and redistribution rights; cannot be estimated responsibly without SFI's use case and vendor quote |

These ranges are early planning heuristics, not a procurement estimate. A vendor/team plan and data-rights quotes are required for a defensible budget. The market-data licence may be a major recurring cost and can constrain which user tiers/features are viable.

## Near-term sequence

1. Confirm the scope assumptions and intended paid customer.
2. Request NGX and FMDQ pricing and written rights for web display, derived data, reports, AI processing and redistribution by subscription tier.
3. Obtain legal review of product boundaries under the ISA 2025 and current SEC rules.
4. Build Phase 1 application foundation, with no fake market values.
5. Start Phase 2 only when a feed is authorized or an observation is clearly sourced, dated and permitted for the intended use.
